Ada Lovelace

A Pioneer of Computing

Ada Lovelace was an English mathematician and writer who became known for her work on Charles Babbage's Analytical Engine.

Her notes about the machine included an algorithm intended to be processed by the Analytical Engine. Her work demonstrated that mechanical computers could have applications beyond numerical calculation.

Lovelace's ideas became an important part of the early history of computing and continue to be remembered in computer science.
