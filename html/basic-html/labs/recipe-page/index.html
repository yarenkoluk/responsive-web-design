Classic Pancakes

A simple and delicious pancake recipe that is perfect for a quick breakfast.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup all-purpose flour
  • 2 tablespoons sugar
  • 1 teaspoon baking powder
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 large egg
  • 2 tablespoons melted butter

Instructions

  1. Combine the flour, sugar, and baking powder in a bowl.
  2. Add the milk, egg, and melted butter.
  3. Mix until the ingredients are just combined.
  4. Heat a lightly greased pan over medium heat.
  5. Pour the batter into the pan and cook until bubbles form.
  6. Flip the pancake and cook until golden brown.
  7. Serve warm with your favorite toppings.
